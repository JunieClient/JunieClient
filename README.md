Kill Aura: With this module, you will be able to hit targets around you at 5cps and wont have to press your mouse once! You can trick your friends to get an unfair advantage or just use it againts mobs in which you will never be hit.




Target Health: Target Health makes it so that you see mobs and players HP: (20 hp = 10 mc hearts) This will give you a sense of awareness on when and when not to attack and how your oppent or how the "mob" is doing!



 
Radar: The Radar does not show an ingame radar (like minimap) but it does show text of the amount of entities in your area, say you are playing hide and seek with a friend or friends and you have radar on. You will know that they are close without giving away their exact position so its still fun.

How to Use:
Download and import v5.41 of JunieClient into your Minecraft Bedrock world.

Make sure Experimental Gameplay features are enabled, required by the script. (toggle BETA API on!)

Use the in-game method provided by the client to open the menu and start tweaking your settings! (Use Compass to open)!
